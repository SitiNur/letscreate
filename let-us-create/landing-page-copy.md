## 5. How Learning Works

Section label:

**Our learning framework**

Headline:

> **Imagine. Explore. Create.**

Intro:

Every session combines a clear learning objective with space for personal choices. Children receive enough structure to move forward—and enough freedom to make the outcome their own.

### Imagine

It starts with an idea. Children choose a character, story, behavior, color, challenge, or possibility they want to explore.

### Explore

They learn the tools and concepts they need by trying, testing, and asking questions. Unexpected results become part of the discovery.

### Create

They turn what they learned into something they can play, present, explain, or share—and leave with the confidence to say, “I made this.”

Supporting statement:

> **Structured enough to guide. Flexible enough to create.**

## 6. What Makes Us Different

Headline:

> **Not just what they know, but what they can create.**

### Create from the first class

Children begin making immediately. A first creation can be small, but the ownership and excitement are real.

### Projects, not passive lectures

Concepts are introduced when children can use them in a meaningful project, making learning easier to understand and remember.

### One foundation, many possibilities

The class may begin from the same lesson, but every child can make different creative choices. We do not expect seven identical results.

### Facilitators, not answer machines

Our facilitators ask, listen, demonstrate, and guide. They help children find the next step without taking over the creation.

### Progress children can show

Each project makes learning visible. Children can explain what they made, how it works, and what they want to improve next.

## 14. FAQ

### Does my child need coding experience?

No. Creative Coding: Explorer is designed as an introduction. We begin with age-appropriate tools and concepts, then help each child progress from their own starting point.

### Is this only a coding class?

Coding is our first program, but Let Us Create is built around creative learning. Future programs may include design, digital storytelling, illustration, animation, and other creative technologies.

### Will every child make the same project?

Children may share a learning objective and starting point, but they are encouraged to make personal choices. Their characters, stories, interactions, colors, and final results can be different.

### What will my child learn?

Alongside introductory coding concepts, children practice creative thinking, sequencing, problem-solving, experimentation, persistence, and explaining their work.

### What if my child makes a mistake?

Mistakes are a normal and useful part of creating. Facilitators help children observe what happened, understand why, and decide what they want to try next.

### How large is each class?

Small groups are preferred so facilitators can observe and support each child. Add the confirmed maximum class size before publishing.

### What device or software is required?

Add the selected learning platform, device requirements, and any setup instructions before registration opens.

### How do you protect children's privacy?

Children's work and personal information are handled carefully. Photos, identifiable names, recordings, or published projects require documented guardian consent, with less-identifiable participation options available.
